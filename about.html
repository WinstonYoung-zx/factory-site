About Zhanxin

Founded in 2023 by an industry veteran team, delivering premium metal fabrication and LED display enclosure solutions with 15+ years of cumulative industry expertise.

Who We Are

Engineering Excellence, Collective Expertise

Shanghai Zhanxin Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd. was founded in August 2023. Our core team comes from leading enterprises in the outdoor advertising equipment industry, with over 15 years of cumulative hands-on experience in design, manufacturing and project delivery.

We focus on advertising light boxes, wayfinding signage, large-size LED display enclosures, bus shelters and custom metal structures. Our standard production lead time is 15-30 days EXW. We have established long-term cooperative relations with multiple global top-tier outdoor media groups, and completed advertising facility projects for over 40 metro lines in China during our previous industry tenure.
