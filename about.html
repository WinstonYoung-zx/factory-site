About SHANGHAI ZHANXIN

Reliable manufacturer for outdoor, airport and urban furniture & metal structure projects

Our Story

Founded in August 2023, Shanghai Zhanxin Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd. is a specialized manufacturer of metal structures, advertising light boxes, LED display frames and bus shelters. Our core team previously served global industry leaders. We bring decades of hands‑on engineering & project experience to deliver custom metal fabrication for urban furniture, airport facilities and large screen mounting structures to worldwide clients.
