About Us

Company History

We are a professional manufacturer specializing in urban furniture, metal structures, advertising light boxes, wayfinding signage, bus shelters and digital advertising display equipment. Our factory has rich export experience for Europe, Middle East and North America B2B projects.


Factory & QC Process

We have full production workshop: laser cutting, bending, welding, powder coating, assembly and quality inspection. Every product will go through raw material inspection, production mid-check and finished product test before shipment.


Certifications & OEM / ODM

Available certificates: ISO9001, CE, SGS. We support OEM & ODM, you can send drawings, sketches or reference photos for custom development.
