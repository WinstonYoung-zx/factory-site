Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MOQ, customization, lead time, payment terms, installation, warranty, samples and trade terms.

What is your MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)? ▼
Our standard MOQ is one 20-foot container (20'GP). For sample orders or trial orders, we can discuss flexible solutions based on your specific project requirements.
Can I customize products based on my own drawings? ▼
Yes, we provide full OEM/ODM customization. We can manufacture products strictly according to your drawings and specifications, or we can co-design products together with our engineering team to optimize the solution for your project.
What is your lead time? ▼
Standard production lead time is 25-35 working days after deposit confirmation and drawing approval. The exact timeline depends on product type, quantity and customization complexity. We will provide a detailed production schedule after order confirmation.
What payment terms do you accept? ▼
We accept T/T (Telegraphic Transfer) payment terms. Typically 30% deposit upon order confirmation, 70% balance before shipment.
Do you provide on-site installation service overseas? ▼
Currently we do not provide on-site installation service overseas. However, we offer free remote installation guidance via video call and detailed installation manuals. Paid on-site installation supervision is available for large projects, please contact us for details.
What is the warranty period? ▼
We provide 1-year warranty for the overall product structure (aluminum frame, steel structure, powder coating). LED light source and LED display panels come with a 2-year warranty. During the warranty period, we provide free replacement parts for quality-related issues.
What trade terms do you support? ▼
We support EXW (Ex Works) and FOB (Free On Board) trade terms. Our nearest port is Shanghai Port.
Can you provide samples before mass production? ▼
Yes, we can produce paid samples. Sample cost is quoted separately based on product specifications and complexity. Shipping cost is borne by the buyer.
How do you quote for a custom project? ▼
Each custom project is quoted individually based on product type, dimensions, materials, quantity, customization details and shipping destination. Please send us your drawings or requirements, and we will provide a detailed quotation within 2-3 working days.

Contact us and we will get back to you within 24 hours.
