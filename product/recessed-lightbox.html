FAQ

Can you produce from our drawings?

Yes. Most of our orders are built to customer drawings. Send a DWG, PDF or a dimensioned sketch and we will produce a shop drawing for approval before production.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Sample orders are welcome so you can check the finish and build quality before placing a full order. Production MOQ depends on the model and quantity you need — tell us your project details and we will confirm.

Is the light box suitable for outdoor use?

Yes. Our outdoor units are built to IP65 with sealed gaskets and stainless steel or coated fasteners. Salt spray test reports are available on request.

Can you add our logo?

Yes — printing, engraving or embossing. We provide a mock-up for your approval before production.
