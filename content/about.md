intro: >-
  Shanghai Zhanxin Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd. is a manufacturer of
  advertising light boxes, wayfinding and signage systems, and LED/LCD display
  products, based in Jinshan District, Shanghai, China.


  **Our Story**

  Our founding team spent more than a decade at a long-standing Chinese supplier of advertising equipment structures, serving one of the world's leading outdoor advertising groups as a qualified structural component supplier since 2008. Over those years, our engineers and production staff delivered light box structures, signage components, and media equipment for metro, airport, and street furniture projects across China. In 2022, when the previous company discontinued its manufacturing operations, the whole core team — engineering, production, quality, and project management — made a collective decision to carry on the work independently and founded Zhanxin. Only the company is new — the capability and the people are not.


  **What We Do**

  Backed by our own production facility, we cover the full chain from engineering design and structural fabrication to assembly, testing, and on-site installation. Our portfolio includes advertising light boxes, metro and airport media equipment, bus shelters, directional signage, LED/LCD display structures, and lighting solutions for the outdoor media industry. We support OEM and ODM orders with customized sizes, structures, and branding. Whether you need a single prototype or a full project supply, our factory-based capability helps you shorten lead time and control quality.


  We welcome partners worldwide to request a quotation or arrange a factory visit.
qc: >-
  We apply quality control at every stage of production, from material intake to
  pre-shipment inspection.


  1. **Incoming Quality Control (IQC)**

     ​ — Aluminum, steel, LED modules, power supplies, acrylic panels, and diffusers are inspected for dimensions, finish, and electrical parameters before entering production.
  2. **In-Process Quality Control (IPQC)**

     ​ — Welding, cutting, bending, and coating stages are checked against drawings and tolerances at each step.
  3. **Electrical & Aging Test**

      — LED light sources, power supplies, and control systems undergo aging tests to confirm stability and brightness consistency.
  4. **Structure & Waterproofing Check**

      — Assembly accuracy, sealing, and waterproofing are verified for outdoor and semi-outdoor products.
  5. **Final Quality Control (FQC)**

     ​ — Finished goods are checked for appearance, color consistency, function, and labeling.
  6. **Pre-Shipment Inspection**

      — Packaging, drop and vibration protection, and container loading are confirmed before dispatch.

  Inspection photos and reports can be provided on request.
